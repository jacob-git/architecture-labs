# Code of Conduct

Participation in Architecture Labs should remain respectful, evidence-focused, and open to results that challenge the working hypothesis.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory conduct, deliberate disclosure of private information, and knowingly misleading experimental claims are not acceptable. Discuss the method, implementation, evidence, and limitations—not the worth or motives of contributors.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Proportionate corrective action may include clarification, removal of content, contribution restrictions, or exclusion from the project.
